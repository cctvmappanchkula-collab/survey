How to get Latitude, Longitude

  1. Stand next to the camera and open the Google Maps app.
  2. Tap the blue location button so the map shows where you are.
  3. Long-press on your blue dot. A red pin drops.
  4. The coordinates appear at the top (search bar) or in the bottom panel. Tap them to copy.
  5. Come back here and paste into the Latitude, Longitude box.

It must look like: 30.7333, 76.7794
